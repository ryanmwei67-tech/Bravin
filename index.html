Pro Study Tips

Science-backed strategies that top students actually use.

🧠
Spaced Repetition
Review material at increasing intervals (1 day → 3 days → 7 days). This fights the forgetting curve and locks info into long-term memory.
✍️
Active Recall
Close your notes and try to remember. Test yourself BEFORE looking at answers. This is 3x more effective than re-reading.
⏱
Pomodoro Technique
Study 25 min → Break 5 min → Repeat. After 4 rounds, take a 15-30 min break. Keeps focus sharp.
🗺
Mind Mapping
Draw visual connections between concepts. Great for subjects like Science & Social Studies where topics interconnect.
📝
Past Papers First
KNEC repeats patterns. Do past papers early, not just before exams. Identify recurring question types and master them.
🗣
Teach Someone
Explain a concept to a friend or sibling. If you can teach it simply, you truly understand it. (Feynman Technique)
😴
Sleep = Memory
Your brain consolidates what you learned during deep sleep. Less than 7 hrs = 40% worse recall. Never sacrifice sleep for study.
🥗
Brain Food
Eat eggs, fish, nuts, fruits, and drink water. Avoid heavy sugar — it causes energy crashes that kill concentration.
📵
Phone Away
Put your phone in another room during study blocks. Even seeing it reduces cognitive capacity by 10%.
🎯
Start Hard, Finish Easy
Tackle your hardest subject first when energy is highest. Save easier tasks for when you're tired.
📊
Track Your Scores
Keep a simple score log for every practice test. Watch your improvement — it's motivating and shows what still needs work.
🧘
Manage Exam Anxiety
Breathe in 4 sec → hold 4 sec → out 6 sec. Do this before exams. A little stress is normal — it keeps you sharp.
